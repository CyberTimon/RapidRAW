RapidRAW creates automatic video captions with the speech recognition model
Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3 by NVIDIA (https://huggingface.co/nvidia/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3).

The model is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
license (CC-BY-4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
RapidRAW downloads the ONNX export by istupakov
(https://huggingface.co/istupakov/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3-onnx) without modifications
when captions are generated for the first time.
